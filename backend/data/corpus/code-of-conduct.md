# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Northwind Systems expects every employee, contractor and board member to act
with honesty, respect and good judgement. This Code sets the minimum standard;
where local law is stricter, local law applies.

## Respect at work

Harassment, bullying, discrimination and retaliation are prohibited. This covers
conduct based on race, colour, national origin, religion, sex, gender identity or
expression, sexual orientation, age, disability, pregnancy, marital status or
veteran status - in the office, at company events, and in any company channel.

If you experience or witness such conduct, report it to your manager, People
Operations, or the confidential ethics line. Reports may be made anonymously.
**Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is itself gross
misconduct.**

## Conflicts of interest

Declare anything that could reasonably be seen to compromise your judgement,
including:

- Outside employment or consulting in the same market
- A financial interest in a supplier, customer or competitor
- A close personal relationship with someone you manage, or who manages you
- Serving on the board of another company

Declarations are made in the People Portal and reviewed by Legal. Declaring is
not an admission of wrongdoing; failing to declare is the violation.

Close personal relationships within a reporting line must be disclosed so the
reporting line can be changed. The relationship itself is not prohibited.

## Anti-bribery and corruption

Never offer, give, request or accept anything of value to improperly influence a
decision. This applies to cash, gifts, hospitality, favours, internships and
charitable donations.

- Gifts and hospitality **above INR 5,000** in value must be declared in the
  gift register within 10 working days.
- **Anything given to or received from a public official requires prior Legal
  approval**, regardless of value.
- Facilitation payments are prohibited without exception, even where locally
  customary.

## Fair competition

Do not discuss pricing, market allocation, bids or hiring intentions with
competitors. Leave any conversation that turns to these topics, and report it to
Legal the same day.

## Company assets and information

Use company assets for company purposes. Incidental personal use of devices is
acceptable; using them for an outside business is not. Confidential information
remains confidential after you leave.

Insider trading rules apply to anyone holding material non-public information:
do not trade, and do not tip others. Designated employees are additionally
subject to closed periods before results.

## Speaking externally

Only authorised spokespeople may speak to media or analysts on behalf of the
company. When posting publicly in a personal capacity about the industry, make
clear that the views are your own, and never disclose non-public information
about products, customers or financials.

## Substance use

Reporting for work impaired by alcohol or drugs is a safety and conduct issue.
Alcohol served at company events must be optional, and a non-alcoholic
alternative must always be available.

## Raising concerns

Speak to your manager first where you can. Where you cannot, use the ethics
line, which is operated by an independent third party and available 24 hours a
day in every language the company operates in. Every report is triaged within
**5 working days**, and the reporter is told the outcome to the extent the law
allows.

## Consequences

Breaching this Code may lead to disciplinary action up to and including
dismissal, and where the law has been broken, referral to the authorities.
