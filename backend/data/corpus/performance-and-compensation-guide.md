# Performance and Compensation Guide

## Performance cycle

Northwind runs two formal review cycles per year:

- **H1 review**: written in June, decisions communicated in July
- **H2 review**: written in December, decisions communicated in January

Both cycles include a self-assessment, peer feedback from three to five
colleagues, and a manager assessment. Self-assessments are due **10 working days
before** the manager assessment deadline.

Between cycles, managers hold a documented check-in at least monthly. Feedback
should never be saved up for the review; nothing in a review should be a
surprise.

## Rating scale

| Rating | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Exceptional | Consistently delivered beyond the level's expectations |
| Strong | Fully met the level's expectations with some standout work |
| Solid | Met expectations |
| Developing | Below expectations in one or more areas; a support plan is agreed |
| Not meeting | Sustained shortfall; a formal improvement plan begins |

There is no forced distribution. Ratings are calibrated across each function to
keep the bar consistent between managers.

## Promotion

Promotion recognises sustained performance at the next level, not potential.
Nominations are submitted in the review cycle by the manager, with evidence
mapped to the level expectations in the career framework. Promotion decisions
are made in calibration, not by a single manager.

Time in level is not a qualifying criterion, though most promotions follow at
least **12 months** at the current level.

## Compensation review

Base pay is reviewed annually, effective 1 April, with decisions based on
performance rating, position in the band, and market movement. Bands are set by
job family, level and location, and are refreshed each January against market
data.

Employees may see their band and position in the band in the People Portal.

## Bonus

The annual company bonus is paid in April with a target of **10% of base salary**
for individual contributors and 15% for managers. Payout is a function of
company performance (70% weighting) and individual rating (30% weighting).
Employees must be employed and not under notice on the payment date to receive
the bonus. New joiners receive a pro-rata bonus based on months served.

## Equity

New hires at level 3 and above receive a restricted stock unit grant vesting
over **4 years with a 1-year cliff**, then quarterly. Refresh grants are
considered annually in the April cycle. Vested equity is unaffected by
resignation; unvested equity is forfeited.

## Referral bonus

A successful referral pays INR 100,000 for engineering roles and INR 60,000 for
other roles, split 50% on the joiner's start date and 50% after they complete
probation.

## Improvement plans

A formal performance improvement plan runs for **8 weeks** with weekly written
check-ins and clearly stated, measurable success criteria. People Operations
reviews every plan before it starts. An employee may bring a colleague to any
formal plan meeting.

## Raising a concern about a review

Employees may request a review of their rating within **10 working days** of
receiving it. The request goes to People Operations, who arrange a review by a
manager outside the reporting line.
