# Remote Work and Hybrid Policy

## Working pattern

Northwind Systems operates a hybrid model. Office-contract employees are
expected on site for a **minimum of 3 days per week**, including the team's
designated anchor day. Anchor days are set by each team lead per quarter and
published in the team calendar.

Remote-contract employees have no on-site requirement but are expected to attend
two company on-sites per year, with travel costs covered by the company.

## Core hours

Teams operate core collaboration hours of **10:00 to 16:00 in the employee's
registered time zone**. Outside core hours, employees manage their own schedule
provided commitments are met. Meetings should not be scheduled outside core
hours without agreement.

## Working from a different location

Working from a location other than your registered one for up to **20 working
days per calendar year** requires manager approval only. Beyond 20 days, or for
any period outside your country of employment, you must obtain approval from
both your manager and People Operations at least **15 working days in advance**,
because of tax residency, immigration and payroll exposure.

Working from a sanctioned jurisdiction is prohibited in all circumstances.

## Home workspace requirements

Employees working remotely must have:

- A reliable internet connection of at least 25 Mbps downstream
- A workspace that allows confidential calls to be taken privately
- A company-issued device with full-disk encryption enabled

The company provides a one-time home office allowance of INR 25,000 for
remote-contract employees. It covers a desk, chair, monitor and peripherals, and
is claimed through the Finance Portal under the "equipment" category with IT
approval attached.

## Equipment and security

Company data may only be accessed from company-managed devices. Personal
devices may be used for calendar and chat via the managed mobile application,
but never for source code, customer data or financial records.

Public Wi-Fi must be used with the corporate VPN. See the Information Security
Policy for the full device standard.

## Health and safety

Remote employees are responsible for maintaining a safe working environment.
A self-assessment checklist must be completed on joining and annually
thereafter. Work-related injuries at a home workspace must be reported to People
Operations within 24 hours, exactly as they would be on site.

## Office attendance exceptions

Employees may request a temporary reduction in on-site days for medical reasons,
caring responsibilities or a documented accessibility requirement. Requests go to
People Operations, not the line manager, and are reviewed within 5 working days.

## Meeting etiquette

Any meeting with at least one remote participant is a remote-first meeting:
everyone joins from their own device, and the agenda and notes are written in the
shared document rather than only spoken. Recording requires the consent of all
participants and must follow the retention rules in the Data Protection Policy.

## Changing your working pattern

A permanent change between office and remote contract is a contractual change.
Submit the request in the People Portal; it requires manager and Director
approval and a minimum of **30 days' notice**, as compensation bands are
location-linked.
