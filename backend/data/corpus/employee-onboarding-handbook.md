# Employee Onboarding Handbook

## Before your first day

People Operations sends your offer pack, background-check consent and equipment
survey once your contract is signed. Complete the equipment survey at least
**10 business days before your start date** so IT can ship your laptop to arrive
three business days early.

Background verification covers identity, right to work, education and the last
two employers. It typically completes in 7 working days.

## Day one

Your manager runs a welcome session at 09:30 local time. By the end of day one
you should have:

- Signed in to the identity portal and enrolled multi-factor authentication
- Access to chat, email, the People Portal and your team's repositories
- Met your onboarding buddy
- Completed the workspace health and safety self-assessment

If any core account is missing, raise a **high** priority IT ticket rather than
waiting.

## First week

Mandatory training must be completed within **10 working days** of joining:
Information Security Essentials, Data Protection, Code of Conduct, and
Anti-Bribery. Each takes 30-45 minutes. Completion is tracked and reported to
your manager.

Your manager will agree your first 30-day goals by the end of week one and
record them in the performance system.

## Probation

Probation is **3 months** for individual contributors and 6 months for people
managers. There is a formal check-in at the halfway point and at the end. During
probation either party may terminate with 2 weeks' notice.

Probation does not restrict access to annual or sick leave; entitlement starts
accruing from day one.

## Pay and benefits enrolment

Salary is paid monthly on the **last working day** of the month. The payroll
cut-off for changes is the 20th.

Benefits enrolment opens on your start date and must be completed within 30
days, after which the next window is the annual enrolment period in November.
The standard package includes private medical cover for you and dependants, life
assurance at 4x salary, an employee assistance programme, and a pension with an
employer contribution of 8% matched to your 4%.

## Your team and ways of working

Every team publishes a working agreement covering anchor days, core hours,
on-call expectations and its definition of done. Ask your buddy for the link in
your first week.

Northwind runs on written communication: decisions are recorded in documents,
not in meetings. Long-form updates are preferred over status meetings.

## Who to ask

| Topic | Where to go |
| --- | --- |
| Pay, leave, benefits, contracts | People Portal request |
| Laptop, access, software | IT Service Desk portal |
| Expenses, travel booking | Finance Portal |
| Security concern or incident | IT Service Desk, critical priority |
| Anything you are unsure about | Your manager or onboarding buddy |

## First 90 days

By day 30 you should be shipping small changes independently. By day 60 you
should own a component or process end to end. By day 90 your manager completes
the probation review and confirms your permanent status in writing.
