# Leave and Time Off Policy

## Purpose and scope

This policy covers all permanent and fixed-term employees of Northwind Systems
worldwide. Contractors and agency staff follow the terms of their engagement
agreement instead. Where local statutory entitlement is more generous than this
policy, the statutory entitlement applies.

## Annual leave entitlement

Every full-time employee receives **24 days of paid annual leave** per calendar
year, accrued at 2 days per completed month of service. Part-time employees
accrue pro rata based on contracted hours.

New joiners may take accrued leave from their first day; they do not need to
complete probation first. Leave taken in excess of the accrued balance is
recovered from final pay if employment ends.

A maximum of **6 unused annual leave days may be carried forward** into the next
calendar year. Carried-forward days expire on 31 March. Days beyond that cap are
forfeited and are not paid out, except where local law requires otherwise.

## Requesting annual leave

All leave is requested through the People Portal. Email or chat requests to a
manager are not a valid submission and will not appear in payroll.

Notice requirements:

- Requests of **3 or more consecutive working days require 10 working days of
  notice** before the first day of leave.
- Requests of **1 or 2 working days require 2 working days of notice**.
- A manager may waive the notice requirement in writing for urgent personal
  circumstances. The waiver is recorded against the request.

Requests longer than **10 consecutive working days** additionally require sign-off
from an HR Business Partner, because of handover and payroll implications.

Managers are expected to respond to a leave request within 3 working days. If a
request is not actioned within 5 working days it is escalated automatically to
the manager's manager.

## Leave that overlaps a blackout period

Each function may declare up to 15 blackout days per year (for example, the
finance close in the first week of April). Annual leave is not normally approved
during a declared blackout period. Blackout calendars are published in the People
Portal at the start of each quarter.

## Sick leave

Employees receive **12 paid sick days** per calendar year. Sick leave does not
carry forward.

Notify your manager before the start of your working day if you are unable to
work. A **medical certificate is required for any sickness absence longer than 2
consecutive working days**, and must be uploaded to the People Portal within 5
working days of returning.

Sickness during booked annual leave may be converted to sick leave on production
of a medical certificate, restoring the annual leave balance.

## Parental leave

- **Maternity leave**: 26 weeks at full pay, available from 8 weeks before the
  expected date of delivery.
- **Paternity and partner leave**: 4 weeks at full pay, to be taken within 6
  months of birth or placement.
- **Adoption leave**: matched to maternity leave terms for the primary carer.

Parental leave requires 30 days of notice where the date is foreseeable.
Entitlement is unaffected by the annual leave balance, and annual leave continues
to accrue throughout parental leave.

## Unpaid leave and sabbaticals

Unpaid leave of up to 20 working days may be granted at the manager's discretion
once the annual leave balance is exhausted. Sabbaticals of up to 3 months are
available after 5 years of continuous service and require Director approval.

## Bereavement and emergency leave

Up to **5 days of paid bereavement leave** for an immediate family member and up
to 2 days for extended family. Emergency dependant leave of up to 2 days per
incident is available for the unexpected care of a dependant, and does not count
against the annual leave balance.

## Public holidays

Public holidays follow the calendar of the employee's registered work location
and are in addition to annual leave. Employees required to work a public holiday
receive a day off in lieu, to be taken within 60 days.

## Cancellation and changes

Approved leave may be cancelled by the employee up to 2 working days before the
start date without approval. Cancellations closer to the start date require
manager confirmation, as cover may already have been arranged.

The company may recall an employee from leave only in a declared incident, and
must reimburse any non-refundable costs.
