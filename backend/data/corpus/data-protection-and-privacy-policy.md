# Data Protection and Privacy Policy

## Why this matters

Northwind Systems processes personal data belonging to employees, customers and
customers' end users. Mishandling it causes real harm to people and exposes the
company to regulatory penalties of up to 4% of global annual turnover.

## Principles

Personal data must be:

1. Processed lawfully, fairly and transparently
2. Collected for a specified purpose and not reused incompatibly
3. Limited to what is necessary for that purpose
4. Accurate and kept up to date
5. Retained no longer than necessary
6. Kept secure against unauthorised access or loss

## Lawful basis

Before processing personal data for a new purpose, record the lawful basis in
the processing register. Consent is the weakest basis and must be freely given,
specific and withdrawable; prefer contract necessity or legitimate interests
where they genuinely apply. A legitimate interests assessment must be documented.

## Data minimisation in practice

Do not copy production data into development or test environments. Use the
synthetic dataset generator. Where production data is unavoidable for debugging,
request a time-boxed approval from the Data Protection Office; access expires
after **7 days** and is logged.

Never paste customer personal data into an unapproved third-party service,
including AI assistants that are not on the approved list.

## Retention

| Data | Retention |
| --- | --- |
| Job applicant records (unsuccessful) | 12 months from decision |
| Employee records | 7 years after employment ends |
| Customer support transcripts | 24 months |
| Security and access logs | 13 months |
| Marketing contact data | 24 months from last engagement |
| Financial records | 7 years, statutory |

Retention is enforced automatically. Do not create shadow copies in personal
drives that escape the schedule.

## Data subject requests

Individuals may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal
data. Forward any such request to the Data Protection Office on the day you
receive it. The statutory deadline is **30 calendar days** from receipt, and the
clock starts when any employee receives the request, not when Legal does.

Do not respond to a data subject request directly, and never delete data in
response to a request without Legal confirming the obligation.

## Personal data breaches

A personal data breach is any loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or
access to personal data - including sending an email to the wrong recipient.

Report to the Data Protection Office **within 24 hours** of becoming aware.
Regulators must be notified within 72 hours where there is a risk to
individuals, so internal speed matters. Include what data, how many people,
when, and what has been done so far.

## International transfers

Transferring personal data outside its region of origin requires an approved
transfer mechanism. Check the approved subprocessor list before sending data to
any vendor, and never add a new subprocessor without Legal and Security review.

## Privacy by design

New features that process personal data require a Data Protection Impact
Assessment when they involve large-scale processing, special category data,
profiling or monitoring. Start the assessment at the design stage; retrofitting
one after launch is a finding in itself.

## Employee monitoring

The company monitors systems for security purposes only, under a documented
policy. Content of private messages is not reviewed except during a formal
investigation authorised by the General Counsel.
