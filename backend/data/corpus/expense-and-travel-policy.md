# Expense and Business Travel Policy

## Principles

Spend company money as if it were your own. Every claim must be a genuine
business expense, reasonable in the circumstances, and supported by evidence.
When a rule does not obviously cover your situation, ask Finance before you
spend rather than after.

## Filing a claim

Claims are filed in the Finance Portal. **Expenses must be claimed within 30
days of the date they were incurred.** Claims older than 30 days are rejected
automatically and require a written exception from the Finance Controller.

An **itemised receipt is mandatory for any single expense above INR 1,000**
(or local equivalent: USD 12, GBP 10, EUR 12). Card statements are not accepted
as receipts because they do not itemise.

Reimbursement is paid with the next payroll run after approval. The payroll
cut-off is the 20th of each month.

## Approval thresholds

| Claim value (INR) | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 10,000 | Automatic approval after policy checks |
| 10,001 - 50,000 | Reporting manager |
| Above 50,000 | Finance Controller |
| Any amount, non-standard category | Finance Controller |

Managers may not approve their own claims; these route one level up.

## Daily allowances

- **Domestic meals**: up to INR 1,200 per day, inclusive of all meals.
- **International meals**: up to USD 60 per day.
- Alcohol is not reimbursable except at a pre-approved client entertainment
  event, where it must be itemised separately.

## Travel booking

All flights, rail and hotels must be booked through the corporate travel desk so
that duty-of-care tracking works. Self-booked travel is reimbursed only up to
the fare the travel desk would have paid.

- **Air travel**: economy class for journeys under 6 hours. Premium economy is
  permitted for journeys over 6 hours; business class requires Director approval.
- **Rail**: standard class for journeys under 3 hours.
- **Hotels**: up to INR 8,000 per night domestically, USD 220 per night
  internationally. Book the negotiated corporate rate where one exists.
- **Ground transport**: standard ride-hailing or public transport. Premium tiers
  are reimbursable only between 22:00 and 06:00 or when carrying equipment.

Book at least **14 days in advance** where the trip is foreseeable. Bookings made
inside 7 days require a documented business reason.

## Mileage and personal vehicles

Use of a personal vehicle for business travel is reimbursed at INR 12 per
kilometre, which covers fuel, wear and insurance. Log start and end odometer
readings in the claim. Commuting between home and your registered office is not
a business expense.

## Equipment and software

Hardware and software purchases are not employee expenses. Raise an IT service
desk request instead so the asset is registered and licensed correctly. Claims
for self-purchased equipment are rejected unless IT has issued a prior written
exception.

Home-office equipment is covered by a one-time allowance of INR 25,000 for
remote-contract employees, claimed under the "equipment" category with IT
approval attached.

## Training and conferences

Course fees, certification exams and conference tickets up to INR 50,000 per
employee per year are approved by the reporting manager. Beyond that, Director
approval is required. Travel to a conference follows the standard travel rules
above.

## Client entertainment

Client meals and events require pre-approval when the total exceeds INR 15,000.
Record the names and organisations of all attendees in the claim; anti-bribery
rules require it. Gifts to public officials are prohibited without Legal
approval, regardless of value.

## What is never reimbursable

- Traffic fines, parking penalties and speeding tickets
- Personal travel insurance beyond the corporate policy
- In-flight entertainment, hotel minibar and spa charges
- Loyalty programme fees
- Expenses already covered by a client or third party

## Fraud and misuse

Knowingly submitting a false claim is gross misconduct and may lead to dismissal
and recovery of funds. Finance samples 5% of claims each month for audit, and
all claims above INR 50,000 are reviewed.
