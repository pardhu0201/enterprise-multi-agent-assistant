# IT Service Desk Guide

## How to reach us

Raise a ticket in the IT Service Desk portal. Chat and email requests are not
tracked and will be redirected. For a suspected security incident, raise a
**critical** ticket immediately and call the security hotline.

## Priorities and response targets

| Priority | Definition | First response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | Production outage, security incident, whole team blocked | 1 hour, 24x7 |
| High | Individual completely unable to work | 4 business hours |
| Normal | Degraded but workable, standard requests | 1 business day |
| Low | Enhancements, non-urgent questions | 3 business days |

Business hours are 09:00-18:00 local time, Monday to Friday, excluding public
holidays.

## Hardware requests

Standard issue is a 14" laptop with 32 GB of memory and a 1 TB disk. A
replacement is available every 36 months, or sooner if the device fails a
diagnostic check.

Accessories from the standard catalogue - monitor, dock, keyboard, mouse,
headset - are approved by IT without manager sign-off. Non-standard hardware
requires manager approval and a business justification in the ticket.

Faulty hardware is replaced within **2 business days** at a staffed office, or
shipped within 5 business days to a remote address. Do not buy replacement
equipment yourself and claim it as an expense; that claim will be rejected.

## Software and licences

Request software through the service catalogue. Anything in the catalogue is
provisioned automatically once your manager approves the cost centre. Software
outside the catalogue requires a security review, which takes up to **10 working
days**, covering data handling, authentication and subprocessor terms.

Licences are reclaimed automatically after 45 days without use.

## Access requests

Access to a system is requested in the portal and approved by the owner of that
system. **Production and privileged access also requires Security team
approval** and is time-boxed; the default grant is 90 days.

Expect access requests to complete within 1 business day once all approvals are
in. Emergency access outside this process requires an on-call incident record.

## Accounts and identity

- Password resets are self-service in the identity portal.
- Account lockouts clear automatically after 15 minutes.
- Lost MFA device: raise a **high** priority ticket; identity is verified over a
  video call with your manager present.
- Name changes and role changes flow automatically from the HR system within 24
  hours of the HR record being updated.

## Onboarding and offboarding

New joiner equipment ships to arrive **3 business days before the start date**,
provided HR raised the joiner record at least 10 business days in advance. Core
accounts are active from 09:00 on day one.

Leaver accounts are disabled at the end of the final shift and deleted after 30
days. Managers should request a mailbox delegation before the leave date if the
mailbox contents are needed.

## Meeting rooms and AV

Room booking is through the calendar system. Report AV faults as a **normal**
ticket with the room name in the summary; rooms with an open fault are marked
unavailable automatically.

## What IT does not support

Personal devices beyond the managed mobile app, personal accounts, home network
equipment, and software purchased outside the procurement process. IT can advise
on home network problems affecting work but cannot administer equipment it does
not own.
